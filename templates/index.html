About these predictions

Predictions use historical match results, team ratings, scoring form, and venue information. Current lineups, injuries, and tactical changes are not included.

Historical evaluation showed weaker performance on World Cup finals than across all competitive matches. These probabilities are estimates, not guarantees.

Results update when the saved dataset and team-state snapshot are refreshed; this app does not fetch live match results.
